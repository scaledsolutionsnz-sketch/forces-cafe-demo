The cafe inside the Gisborne RSA

The Forces Cafe runs out of the RSA on Childers Road, and that shapes everything about it. The poppy on the door means the tables fill with returned service folk, whanau, workers on smoko and anyone after a decent feed.

You do not need to be a member. Come in for a coffee, stay for lunch, and if there is a band in the courtyard, stay longer.

  • Everyone welcome, member or not
  • Locally run, part of the RSA community
  • Catering and function bookings by arrangement

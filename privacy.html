Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

This website does not have any forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. If you call or email us, we only receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number and what your enquiry is about.

How we use your information

We use contact details solely to respond to your enquiry, arrange bookings or catering, and follow up where you have asked us to. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Third-party services

This site embeds a Google Map and links out to Facebook, Instagram and Google. Those services may collect usage data under their own privacy policies when you interact with them. We recommend reviewing their policies if you have concerns.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own cookies. Embedded services such as Google Maps may set cookies of their own.

Storage and security

Emails and messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Call us on 021 222 1737 and we will sort it out.

Contact

Privacy questions can be raised with us directly at The Forces Cafe, Gisborne RSA, 184 Childers Road, Gisborne 4010.
